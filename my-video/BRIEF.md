## Intent

A premium, cinematic product commercial for the PHOENIX PLAST children's lunch
box — Apple-style product-ad tone: luxury studio lighting, slow deliberate
camera moves, glossy reflections, ultra photorealistic. No narration; the
product and its motion carry the message. Ends on the PHOENIX PLAST logo with
the line "Premium Lunch Box".

## Assets

- assets/images/product-04.jpeg — closed lunch box, angled hero shot (light-blue latches) — primary hero/rotation shot.
- assets/images/product-01.jpeg — lid open, tray with utensils — for the lock/latch macro beat.
- assets/images/product-03.webp — open box filled with chicken, rice, broccoli, carrots, fruit/cheese bites — for the "fresh lunch placed inside" beat.
- assets/images/product-02.png — PHOENIX PLAST gold phoenix logo + wordmark on white — for the closing card.

## Customizations

- Exact product geometry/colors/locks/transparent lid must read as unchanged across all shots (same reference product, not a redesign).
- Scene 1: luxury white studio bg, slow 360° rotation feel, soft daylight, premium reflections, 85mm-lens look.
- Scene 2: push-in macro on the two side locks closing (visual click emphasis — sound-effect catalog unavailable offline, so the "click" is sold visually with a snap/settle motion + light flash, not audio).
- Scene 3: fresh lunch (veg, sandwich, fruit) presented inside the box, bright/family-friendly.
- Scene 4: box closes, hero shot on glossy white surface.
- Ending: PHOENIX PLAST logo + "Premium Lunch Box" wordline.

## Notes

- No HeyGen sign-in and no local TTS/music engine deps in this environment (offline, network egress restricted) — this is a **silent** build: no voiceover, no BGM, no catalog SFX. Motion and light sell the "click" and the reveal instead.
- Studio's live board isn't reachable from the user's browser in this sandboxed session; plan/build review happens via chat + rendered screenshots instead of the live board (storyboard: no).
